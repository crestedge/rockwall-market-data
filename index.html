Common questions about the Rockwall County market

What is the housing market like in Rockwall County, Texas?

In July 2026, Median sale prices across the county ranged from $340,713 in Royse City to $836,934 in Heath. Fate was the tightest market at 5.3 months of supply, and McLendon-Chisholm the softest at 8.6. Rockwall County covers the cities of Rockwall, Heath, Fate, McLendon-Chisholm, and Royse City, and the markets differ enough that a county-wide average hides more than it shows.

Which city in Rockwall County is the most affordable?

Royse City had the lowest median sale price in the county in July 2026 at $340,713. Heath had the highest at $836,934.

Where does this Rockwall County market data come from?

NTREIS, the North Texas Real Estate Information Systems MLS, delivered through 10K Research and InfoSparks. How to read these numbers

Median sale price
The midpoint of closed sale prices. Half of homes sold for more, half for less. Less distorted by a few very expensive sales than the average.
Average sale price
The mean of all closed sale prices in the month.
Median price per square foot
Median closed sale price divided by finished square footage. Useful for comparing homes of different sizes.
Closed sales
Transactions that funded and recorded during the month.
Pending sales
Homes that went under contract during the month. A leading indicator of closings one to two months out.
New listings
Homes that came on the market during the month.
Homes for sale
Active inventory at month end.
Months of supply
How long it would take to sell every active listing at the current sales pace. Under roughly four months favors sellers, four to six is balanced, above six favors buyers.
Median days on market
Midpoint of days from listing to accepted contract. It does not include the time from contract to closing.
Average days on market
Mean days from listing to accepted contract.
Percent of original list price received
Closed price as a share of the first asking price. Below 100 percent means sellers are generally taking less than they first asked.

Figures come from NTREIS, the North Texas Real Estate Information Systems MLS, delivered through 10K Research and InfoSparks, and cover residential closed transactions. Data runs from January 2021 through July 2026 and is refreshed monthly. Numbers for the most recent month can revise slightly as late filings post.
