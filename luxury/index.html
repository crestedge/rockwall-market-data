Common questions about the Rockwall County Luxury market

What is the median home price in Rockwall County Luxury?

The median sale price in the Rockwall County luxury segment was $948,606 in July 2026, up 14.2 percent from $830,742 in July 2025. Median means half of homes sold for more and half for less.

How long does it take to sell a house in Rockwall County Luxury?

Homes in the Rockwall County luxury segment took a median of 34 days on market in July 2026, essentially unchanged from 34. Days on market measures listing to accepted contract, so add roughly 30 to 45 days for closing.

Is Rockwall County Luxury a buyer's market or a seller's market?

The Rockwall County luxury segment had 7.1 months of supply in July 2026, which is a buyer’s market by the conventional reading: under about four months favors sellers, four to six is balanced, above six favors buyers. There were 337 homes for sale at the end of the month.

How many homes sold in Rockwall County Luxury in July 2026?

42 homes closed in the Rockwall County luxury segment during July 2026, down 10.6 percent from 47.

Are sellers in Rockwall County Luxury getting their asking price?

Sellers in the Rockwall County luxury segment received a median of 95.3% of their original list price in July 2026. That means the typical home closed below its first asking price, so there is room to negotiate, particularly on listings that have been sitting.

How to read these numbers

Median sale price
The midpoint of closed sale prices. Half of homes sold for more, half for less. Less distorted by a few very expensive sales than the average.
Average sale price
The mean of all closed sale prices in the month.
Median price per square foot
Median closed sale price divided by finished square footage. Useful for comparing homes of different sizes.
Closed sales
Transactions that funded and recorded during the month.
Pending sales
Homes that went under contract during the month. A leading indicator of closings one to two months out.
New listings
Homes that came on the market during the month.
Homes for sale
Active inventory at month end.
Months of supply
How long it would take to sell every active listing at the current sales pace. Under roughly four months favors sellers, four to six is balanced, above six favors buyers.
Median days on market
Midpoint of days from listing to accepted contract. It does not include the time from contract to closing.
Average days on market
Mean days from listing to accepted contract.
Percent of original list price received
Closed price as a share of the first asking price. Below 100 percent means sellers are generally taking less than they first asked.

Figures come from NTREIS, the North Texas Real Estate Information Systems MLS, delivered through 10K Research and InfoSparks, and cover residential closed transactions. Data runs from January 2021 through July 2026 and is refreshed monthly. Numbers for the most recent month can revise slightly as late filings post.
